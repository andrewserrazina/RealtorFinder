FAQ

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before joining the buyer waitlist.

Does using a buyer's agent cost me anything? +
In most US states, the buyer's agent commission is paid by the home seller as part of the transaction. RealtorFinder is also free for buyers to use. You get professional representation at no direct cost to you.
Are the agents on the platform licensed? +
Every agent on RealtorFinder is verified as a licensed real estate agent before they can submit proposals. We check credentials so you don't have to.
What if I don't like any of the proposals? +
No obligation. You can decline all proposals, wait for more, or simply close your request at any time. There's never any pressure to commit.
How is this different from Zillow or Realtor.com? +
Those platforms show you listings. RealtorFinder finds you the best agent to help you buy — then that agent helps you find the right home. It's the representation layer those sites are missing.
