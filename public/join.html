I already pay for Zillow Premier Agent. Why switch?
You don't have to. On Zillow you compete with 10–20 agents for a lead who may not be ready. On RealtorFinder, the seller posted their own listing — intent is higher. And you pitch with a full proposal, not just a headshot.
Won't this push commissions down?
No. Sellers pick based on the full proposal — your marketing plan, track record, and personal note carry as much weight as commission. The best agents win, not the cheapest.
