Common questions

Straight Answers

I Already Pay for Zillow Premier Agent. Why Switch?
You don't have to switch — RealtorFinder is additive. On Zillow you compete with 10–20 agents for a lead who might not be ready. On RealtorFinder, the seller posted their own listing. Intent is higher and you pitch with a full proposal, not just a headshot.
Won't This Push Commissions Down?
No. Sellers pick based on the full proposal — your marketing plan, track record, and personal note matter as much as commission. The best agents consistently win, not the cheapest.
How Many Sellers Are on the Platform Right Now?
Seller listings go live in August 2026 — that's exactly why now is the right time to join. Founding members build their profile, lock in their market, and get 24-hour early access to every listing the moment it's posted. The realtors who join now will have a meaningful head start over anyone who waits.
What Does It Cost After 2 Months?
There are four plans to choose from: Pay Per Lead at $15/lead (no subscription), Basic at $99/month (10 proposals/month), Professional at $249/month (unlimited proposals + priority access), and Firm at $499/location/month for brokerages and teams. No long-term contracts — cancel any time.
