You don't have to switch — RealtorFinder is additive. The difference: on Zillow you compete with 10–20 agents for a lead who might not even be ready. On RealtorFinder, the seller posted their own listing. They're ready. And you pitch with a full proposal, not just a headshot.

No. Sellers pick based on the full proposal — your marketing plan, track record, and personal note carry as much weight as commission. The best agents consistently win, not the cheapest ones.
