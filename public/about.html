The Problem We Solved

The old way of getting listings is broken

Most realtors spend 60–70% of their time on prospecting — cold calling, door knocking, buying expensive leads — just to land listings that may never convert. It's expensive, demoralizing, and inefficient.

RealtorFinder flips the model. Instead of chasing sellers, you browse a live feed of motivated homeowners who are actively seeking representation right now. You pitch them. They choose you. No gatekeepers.

The old way

  • Cold call strangers hoping they're thinking of selling
  • Pay for lead lists that go cold immediately
  • Compete based on who you know, not what you offer
  • Spend thousands on marketing with no guaranteed ROI
  • Settle for any listing just to keep the pipeline full

The RealtorFinder way

  • Browse real listings from sellers actively seeking agents
  • Submit proposals only on listings you want to win
  • Compete on your track record, rate, and strategy
  • Flat monthly fee — no commission splits, ever
  • Build a pipeline of quality listings, not cold maybes
