The problem we're solving

Right now, sellers list with whoever called them first or whoever their neighbor used. There's no transparent way to compare agents. Most homeowners interview one agent, maybe two.

We're flipping that. Sellers post on RealtorFinder and receive proposals from licensed agents in their area. Commission rate, marketing strategy, experience, reviews — all visible side by side. They choose on the full picture.

For realtors, that means competing on the things you're actually good at. No more cold calling expired listings. No more fighting Zillow for leads. You pitch motivated sellers directly, on your terms.
