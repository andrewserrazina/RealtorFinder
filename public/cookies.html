This Cookie Policy explains how RealtorFinder, LLC ("RealtorFinder," "we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on RealtorFinder.net. By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners. Cookies can be "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent" cookies (remaining on your device for a set period).

3. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are strictly necessary for the Platform to function. They enable core features such as authentication, security, and session management. Without these cookies, you cannot log in or use the Platform's authenticated features. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our cookie settings (though you can block them in your browser settings, which will prevent you from using the Platform).

4. Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember your choices and settings to provide a personalized experience. These include your A/B test variant assignment and any referral codes. These cookies do not collect personally identifiable information and are used solely to improve your experience on our Platform.

5. Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how our Platform is used. We use:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Collects anonymized data about page views, user journeys, and engagement. We use this to measure the performance of different features and marketing campaigns. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Records anonymized session replays and generates heatmaps showing where users click and scroll. This data is anonymized and does not identify individual users. Microsoft Privacy Policy.

6. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies, delete existing cookies, and set preferences for certain websites. The methods vary by browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Opt out of Google Analytics: You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent your data from being sent to Google Analytics.

Opt out of Microsoft Clarity: You can opt out of Microsoft Clarity by visiting clarity.microsoft.com/optout.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Platform. In particular, blocking session cookies will prevent you from logging in.

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Our Platform does not currently respond to DNT signals. However, you can use the opt-out methods described above to limit tracking.
