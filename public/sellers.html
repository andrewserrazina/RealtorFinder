FAQ

Common questions

Is it really free for sellers?
Yes, completely free. Creating an account, posting your listing, receiving proposals, messaging agents, and choosing your agent all cost nothing. The platform is paid for by realtors who subscribe to access your listing.
How do I know the agents are legitimate?
Every agent on RealtorFinder has a verified email and profile. Many have verified license numbers. You can also read reviews left by other sellers who worked with them — every review is tied to an actual listing transaction.
Can I negotiate with agents before accepting?
Absolutely. If you like an agent's proposal but want a lower commission rate, you can send a counter-proposal directly in the platform. The agent can accept, decline, or respond with a new offer.
What happens after I accept a proposal?
You'll receive the agent's contact details and they'll be notified immediately. All other pending proposals for your listing are automatically declined. From there, you work with your chosen agent directly to finalize the listing agreement.
Can I list multiple properties?
Yes. Your seller account supports multiple listings, each with their own set of proposals and activity. Manage everything from one dashboard.
What if I don't receive any proposals?
Listings with detailed descriptions, accurate pricing, and photos consistently attract more proposals. If you're not getting responses, try updating your listing details or reach out — we're happy to help you optimize it.
