Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Is it really free for sellers?
Yes — completely free. Listing your home, receiving proposals, messaging agents, and choosing your agent costs nothing. RealtorFinder is paid for by the agents who subscribe to access your listing.
What is RealtorFinder and how does it work?
RealtorFinder is a reverse marketplace for home sales. Instead of calling agents and hoping for the best, you post your home and agents compete for the listing by sending you proposals. You compare their commission rates, read their reviews, and choose the one you want — all without leaving the platform.
How do I know the agents are legitimate?
All agents on RealtorFinder have verified accounts with license numbers and real seller reviews tied to actual transactions. Many have their license verified directly by our team. You can read reviews from other sellers before you ever commit.
What happens after I accept a proposal?
You'll receive the agent's contact details and they'll be notified right away. All other pending proposals on your listing are automatically declined. From there, you work with your chosen agent to finalize the listing agreement.
I'm a realtor — how do I sign up?
Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial — no credit card required. Browse listings, submit proposals, and grow your business with no commission splits. See pricing →
Do I have to commit to anything when I list?
No. You can review proposals, message agents, and browse at your own pace. You only move forward when you find an agent you want to work with. There's no obligation at any step.
