Our Story

Why We Built RealtorFinder

We saw a broken system — and built a better one.

"

We started RealtorFinder after watching family members get pressured into signing with the first agent who knocked on their door — without any real way to compare options or understand what they were agreeing to. Sellers deserved a marketplace. Agents deserved a level playing field. So we built one.

RF

The RealtorFinder Team

Founded 2024 · Springfield, MA

🔍

The problem we saw

Agents were paying thousands of dollars for cold leads from aggregators — only to compete on name recognition and ad spend. Sellers had no way to see who was actually the best agent for their property. Both sides lost.

⚖️

The fix we built

We flipped the model. Sellers post their listing. Agents come to them — with real proposals, transparent track records, and honest commission rates. No cold calls. No wasted ad spend. Win on merit.
