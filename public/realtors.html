How the Bidding Works

From browsing to closing — here's exactly how you win listings on RealtorFinder.

01
Browse Active Listings

See sellers in your area who are actively looking for representation. Filter by city, price range, and property type to find the best fits for your business.

02
Submit Your Proposal

Send your commission rate, marketing strategy, and credentials directly to the seller. No middleman, no gatekeeping — your pitch goes straight to the decision-maker.
