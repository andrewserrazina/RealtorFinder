Why We Built This

The traditional model puts sellers last

In the traditional real estate process, sellers are passive. You wait for an agent to knock on your door, rely on whoever your neighbor used, and often sign with the first person who calls — with no real way to compare your options.

RealtorFinder flips the model. You post your home. Qualified realtors in your area submit competing proposals — their commission rate, marketing plan, and track record. You compare and choose. No pressure, no obligation.

The old way

  • Cold-called by agents you didn't ask to hear from
  • No easy way to compare agents side by side
  • Commission rates feel fixed and non-negotiable
  • Settle for whoever is most persistent
  • Sign a contract before you know if it's a good fit

The RealtorFinder way

  • List your home on your terms — takes 5 minutes
  • Receive proposals from multiple qualified agents
