Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel any time? +
Yes — cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. You'll retain access through the end of your billing period with no further charges.
How does the 30-day free trial work? +
Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial — no credit card required. You get full access to all features in your chosen plan from day one. At the end of 30 days, you'll be prompted to add a payment method to continue. If you don't, your account pauses with no charge.
What's the difference between Basic and Professional? +
Basic caps you at 10 proposals/month, which works well for agents testing the waters. Professional removes that cap and adds priority alerts, enhanced profile features, and a Featured Agent badge that makes you stand out to sellers.
How does the Firm plan work for multiple locations? +
The Firm plan is priced per location at $499/month each. You get unlimited agents and bids at every location, plus a shared firm dashboard with team reporting and bulk bid tools.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Yes. You can change plans from your account dashboard at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept? +
All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), as well as Apple Pay and Google Pay where supported. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.
How does Pay Per Lead work? +
Sign up free — no credit card required. You can browse all active seller listings immediately. When you find one worth pursuing, pay $15 to unlock the full contact details for that lead. There's no monthly fee, no proposals cap, and no commitment. You can upgrade to a subscription plan any time, and unused lead credits carry over.
